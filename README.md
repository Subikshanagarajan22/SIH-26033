## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Detailed explanation:A direct farmer-to-buyer digital marketplace with logistics support and AI-driven forecasting and routing, built to increase farmers earnings and reduce consumer prices.
2. How it addresses the problem:Removing middlemen lets farmers earn a larger share of the price and lets consumers pay less. AI-based demand forecasting helps farmers plan what to grow and how much, which reduces wastage.
3. Innovation and uniqueness: Direct farmer-to-buyer connection, AI demand forecasting, route optimization for deliveries, transparent pricing (the farmer's share is visible on every order), and regional language support.


## Technical Approach
1. Technologies to be used:
   - Frontend: React or HTML/CSS/JS (mobile-friendly)
   - Backend: Python (Flask/FastAPI) or Node.js
   - Database: MongoDB or PostgreSQL
   - AI/ML: Python with scikit-learn or Prophet for demand forecasting; Google Maps API or OR-Tools for route optimization
   - Payments: UPI / Razorpay

## Feasibility and Viability

1. Feasibility: Uses low-cost, proven technology, and the existing FPO network makes farmer onboarding easy.
2. Challenges and risks: Low digital literacy, poor connectivity, and perishable goods.
3. Strategies: Regional language and voice support, offline/SMS access, cold-chain logistics partners, and a pilot launch in one district.

## Impact and Benefits
1. Potential impact on the target audience: Farmers and FPOs earn more by selling directly, consumers get fresh produce at lower prices, and bulk buyers get a reliable supply.
2. Benefits of the solution:
   - Social: Better market access and stronger rural livelihoods.
   - Economic: Fair pricing and lower intermediary costs.
   - Environmental: Less food wastage and lower fuel use through optimized routes.
